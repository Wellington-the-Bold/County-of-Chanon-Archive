# 简体中文介绍
## 香农伯国档案

County of Chanon（香农伯国）是一个以15至16世纪欧洲为背景的架空历史与制度世界观项目。目前档案主要围绕 **1476—1534年** 展开：从普略特·德·香农（Pruitt de Chanon）建立伯国，到雷蒙二世（Raymond II de Chanon）时期的城市扩张、1507年“三城十二日之乱”与此后的重建，再到1528—1534年的伯位继承战争及伊莱娅继位。

本项目尝试从政治史、城市社会、法律制度、贸易经济、公共工程与人物经历等不同角度，共同构建一个能够彼此衔接的架空历史世界。

## 档案内容

本仓库目前主要收录：

- **历史简述**：伯国历史、政治变动、社会冲突及重要事件；
- **历史法律文件**：敕令、和议、规条及其他历史性法律文本；
- **制度与法律草稿**：宪制、司法、行政、财政及其他制度设计；
- **衍生作品**：以香农伯国世界观为背景创作的小说及其他叙事文本。

目前较为确定的历史框架主要集中于 **1476—1534年**。此后的宪制、法律与行政体系，包括《自治宪章》及其相关法典，仍处于持续修改和完善阶段。

# English Introduction

County of Chanon is an alternate-history and institutional worldbuilding project set against the background of 15th- to 16th-century Europe. The archive currently focuses mainly on the period **1476–1534**: from the founding of the County by **Pruitt de Chanon**, through the urban expansion under **Raymond II de Chanon**, the **Twelve Days' Revolt of the Three Cities in 1507** and the reconstruction that followed, to the **War of Succession of 1528–1534** and the accession of Elijah.

The project attempts to construct a coherent fictional historical world through multiple perspectives, including political history, urban society, legal institutions, trade and economy, public works, and the lives of individual characters.

## Archive Contents

This repository currently contains:

- **Historical Overviews**: general history of the County, political developments, social conflicts, and major events;
- **Historical Legal Documents**: decrees, accords, regulations, and other historical-style legal texts;
- **Institutional and Legal Drafts**: constitutional, judicial, administrative, financial, and other institutional designs;
- **Derivative Works**: fiction and other narrative works set within the world of the County of Chanon.

The comparatively established historical framework currently focuses on **1476–1534**. Later constitutional, legal, and administrative materials, including the **Magna Carta** and its related statutes, remain under continued revision and development.

## Reading and Formatting Notes

This project is primarily written, organized, and maintained in **Obsidian**, and synchronized to GitHub through Git.

Because some documents make use of Obsidian-specific Markdown syntax, internal links, Callouts, embeds, and other extended features, their appearance on GitHub may not fully match their appearance in Obsidian. Some links, layouts, or special formatting may therefore fail to render correctly.

The author is gradually adjusting the document structure and Markdown syntax in order to improve compatibility between Obsidian and GitHub. During this process, formatting issues, broken links, or other display problems do not necessarily indicate that content is missing from the original documents.

If this is your first time exploring the County of Chanon, it is recommended that you begin with the historical overview and then continue according to the events, characters, institutions, or legal documents that interest you. Most archive entries may also be read independently.

## Project Status and Disclaimer

> **This project remains a Work in Progress.**

Texts, chronologies, characters, place names, institutions, and legal provisions in this repository may be expanded, removed, rewritten, or redated as the worldbuilding develops. Where contradictions arise between different documents concerning dates, terminology, institutional arrangements, or character histories, the more recently revised version should generally be treated as authoritative. Current materials should not be regarded as final canon.

This project is intended primarily as an exercise in **alternate history, institutional design, and narrative worldbuilding**. Much of its political, economic, legal, and administrative content is fictional and speculative. It is not intended to reconstruct real historical institutions, nor does it constitute legal, political, economic, or academic advice.

The author is **not a professional historian, legal scholar, or economist**. Some institutional designs, chronological assumptions, legal provisions, and historical reasoning may contain inaccuracies, inconsistencies, or elements that do not fully correspond to real historical conditions. Relevant materials will continue to be revised as further research and worldbuilding development take place.

If you notice timeline conflicts, institutional inconsistencies, historical problems, formatting errors, or anything else that could be improved, you are welcome to submit an issue through **GitHub Issues**. Thank you for your attention and feedback.
