# 简体中文介绍
## County-of-Chanon-Archive

County of Chanon（香农伯国） 是一个以15至16世纪欧洲为背景的架空历史与制度世界观项目。目前档案主要围绕 1476—1534年 展开：从普略特·德·香农 (Pruitt de Chanon) 建立伯国，到雷蒙二世 (Raymond II de Chanon ) 时期的城市扩张、1507年“三城十二日之乱”与此后的重建，再到1528—1534年的伯位继承战争及伊莱娅继位。

# English Introduction

## County-of-Chanon-Archive

**County of Chanon** is an alternate-history and institutional worldbuilding project set against the background of 15th- to 16th-century Europe. The archive currently focuses primarily on the period **1476–1534**: from the founding of the County by **Pruitt de Chanon**, through the urban expansion of the reign of **Raymond II de Chanon**, the **Twelve Days' Revolt of the Three Cities in 1507** and the reconstruction that followed, to the **War of Succession of 1528–1534** and the accession of **Elijah**.

## Notes / Disclaimer

> **This project remains a Work in Progress.**
>
> This repository contains a developing archive of the fictional world of the **County of Chanon**. At present, the comparatively established historical framework is concentrated mainly on the period **1476–1534**. Later materials concerning the constitution, legal system, administration, and related institutions, including the **Magna Carta** and its associated statutes, should still be regarded as drafts subject to further revision.
>
> Texts, chronologies, characters, place names, institutions, and legal provisions contained in this repository may be expanded, removed, rewritten, or redated as the setting develops. Where contradictions arise between different documents concerning dates, terminology, institutional arrangements, or character histories, the more recently revised version should generally be treated as authoritative. Current materials should not be regarded as final canon.
>
> This project is intended primarily as an exercise in **alternate history, institutional design, and narrative worldbuilding**. Its political, economic, legal, and administrative content is largely fictional and speculative. It is not intended as a reconstruction of real historical institutions, nor does it constitute legal, political, economic, or academic advice.
>
> The author is **not a professional historian, legal scholar, or economist**. Some institutional designs, chronological assumptions, legal provisions, or historical arguments may contain inaccuracies, inconsistencies, or elements that do not fully correspond to real historical conditions. Relevant materials may continue to be corrected and refined as further research and worldbuilding development take place. Feedback and corrections are welcome.
